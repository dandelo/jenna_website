What to look for

Perseids

Fast, bright meteors from debris left by Comet 109P/Swift-Tuttle. The radiant climbs well for UK viewers, so this is one of the most reliable annual showers to watch.

Best UK window

22:30-05:00 BST

Wednesday 12 into Thursday 13 August 2026. After midnight is strongest.

Active dates

17 Jul-24 Aug

The shower builds through late July and early August, then falls away after the peak.

Visibility note

New Moon skies

The Moon is new on 12 August, so 2026 has unusually good dark-sky conditions.

Make the most of it

Before 22:30

Find proper darkness

Choose somewhere safe, away from street lights. A wide view of the north-east helps.

22:30 BST

The watch window opens

Perseid meteors can appear anywhere in the sky, so get comfortable and avoid phone glare.

After midnight

Settle in for the best bit

The radiant climbs higher as the night goes on. No telescope needed: a wide, patient view is better.
